Iron farm

886 iron an hour · 812 blocks · 1.1 an hour per block · 226 iron to build · 2 modules in a row share one collection

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

In rain it makes 879 iron an hour and in thunder 823, against 879 on a clear day. Lightning breaks it about once every 634 game days (211 hours of play), most likely by turning a villager into a witch.

93% of the game's maximum · a golem every 16 s · needs 8 villagers, 8 beds and 2 zombies · starts again by itself after a missed night · fits in 41×35×16

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The columns are where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks: blue until one does, then yellow to red by how many have, and a flash at each spawn (Spawn spots hides them). The chest tagged OUT collects the first copy's iron; every copy has its own chest under its last hopper. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: one hopper line under every chamber carries every drop to one sorter, one iron-block crafter and one composter.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Comes loaded with 105 iron ingots and 16 paper and 105 poppy in its containers: the download includes them; building it by hand, put them in first. Pour in the water and the lava (8 bucketfuls) where the view shows them.

Blocks used: chest comparator composter crafter glass hopper lava oak boat oak fence gate oak trapdoor observer red bed redstone torch redstone wire repeater stone stone slab villager water

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT collects the first copy's iron; every copy has its own chest under its last hopper. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
