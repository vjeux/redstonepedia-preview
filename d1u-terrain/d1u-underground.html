Iron farm

hide the zombie by hand the first night · 295 blocks

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

fits in 6×19×16

Iron farmIron farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

Try it

Press play: the view runs the farm as our test runs it, so the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The columns are where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks: blue until one does, then yellow to red by how many have, and a flash at each spawn (Spawn spots hides them). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. Build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Day 1 (candidate D1U): D4 dug into the ground, natural rock for every wall: the bed plate hides the zombie from two scared villagers 6 below the surface; golems fall from the pad into a pit and burn under one lava source.

How to try it

Press play to run the farm: the 3D view runs our test's own simulation of the game's mob rules, the very run our test made first, tick for tick. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower.
