Day-1 iron farm (the cheapest to build)

295 iron an hour · 62% of the ceiling · 189 blocks · 1.6 per block · 11 iron · golems burn in lava pits

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Uses a tripwire: anything that crosses the string (a mob, a player, a dropped item) adds a pulse, so enclose it.

74 golems an hour, one every 49 s, at the game's normal speed · 3 villagers and a zombie · hide the zombie by hand the first night · fits in 16×7×10

How to try it

Press play to run the farm: the 3D view runs our test's own simulation of the game's mob rules, the very run our test made first, tick for tick. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Our test also ran every build in the real game, through days of rain and thunder; its numbers are in the measurements below. On the first night, hide the zombie from the villagers by hand (a block in front of it) and take the block away in the morning: the villagers need one night's sleep before they summon the first golem. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Measurements

Measured by our test, on Hard with the weather's rain, thunder and lightning: the farm built facing all 4 ways at 2 spots in the world, each build run 8 times for 5 game days from the farm's steady state, and every build run in the real game for 5 game days, a day of rain and a day of thunder among them, at the same rate. gt = game tick = 1/20 s (20 gt = 1 second).

Notes from its designer

The day-1 iron farm: for 11 iron (the shears for its leaves counted) and no quartz, three villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems that burn in lava pits, about 295 iron an hour (62% of the game's ceiling). Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell pods under leaves; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes. Golems spawn in a row of 8 1x1 pits west of the zombie, a lava source beside their head on both sides, and burn where they stand. One tripwire runs through every pit at knee height: a golem in any pit opens the trapdoor in front of the zombie, which hides it, and the villagers calm down (at night they fall asleep) while the golem burns. A water channel under the string carries the iron to one hopper. Hide the zombie by hand on the first night: the villagers need a recent sleep to summon the first golem.
