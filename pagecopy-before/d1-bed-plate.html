Iron farm

hide the zombie by hand the first night · 338 blocks

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

fits in 8×22×16

Iron farmIron farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

Try it

Press play: the view runs the farm as our test runs it, so the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The columns are where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks: blue until one does, then yellow to red by how many have, and a flash at each spawn (Spawn spots hides them). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. Build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Day 1 (candidate): the bed-plate calm; golems spawn on a pad 6 below the villagers, fall through its center into a 4 x 4 pit and burn in its corner under one lava source, over one hopper.

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm: the 3D view runs our test's own simulation of the game's mob rules, the very run our test made first, tick for tick. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. It has not been judged yet: its judge is still running (the page goes up again with its numbers). Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
