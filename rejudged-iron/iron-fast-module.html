Fastest iron farm (the most iron for each group of villagers; copies scale it)

459 iron an hour · 96% of the ceiling · 623 blocks · 0.74 per block · 18 iron · golems fall out of sight to a lava blade

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Uses daylight detectors as its dusk clock: they need open sky above them. Rain darkens the sky like dusk and can make them act early; our test runs in clear weather.

How to try it

Press play to run the farm: the 3D view runs our test's own simulation of the game's mob rules, the very run our test made first, tick for tick. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Notes from its designer

FAILED the real-game gate: lava's random-tick fire burns the chamber's oak fence gates by about gt 2000, the lava floods and burns the drops; rainy dusks and thunder stop the dusk pulse. Taken out of the archive; the late stage's stacks replace it. The fastest iron farm module: 459 iron an hour (96% of the game's ceiling) for 18 iron (the tools counted) and 6 quartz; build copies 27 blocks apart to scale it (iron-scaled-2x2: 4 copies, 1836 iron an hour). Four villagers sleep on beds in closed 1-cell pods under leaves (only three must agree, so a golem one of them sees does not stretch the cycle); a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in their view. At nightfall two inverted daylight detectors open the three trapdoors in front of the zombie for 46 game ticks: the villagers fall asleep, then wake up scared for the rest of the night (the farm restarts by itself after a missed night). Golems spawn on an 8x8 pad; water from its corners pushes them onto the hole's ring (water over open fence gates: it holds the water, not a golem) and into its dry 2x2 center (open fence gates stop the water), and they fall 16 blocks out of every villager's sight to water that pushes them under a lava blade held by fence gates, a ceiling right above it, over two hoppers.
