Day-1 iron farm (iron armour on your first day)

295 iron an hour · 189 blocks · 1.6 an hour per block · 11 iron to build · golems burn in lava pits

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

In rain it makes 293 iron an hour and in thunder 299, against 305 on a clear day. Lightning breaks it about once every 2,871 game days (957 hours of play), most likely by a fire. After you have been away for 3 game days, it does not start again by itself.

62% of the game's maximum · a golem every 49 s · needs 3 villagers, 3 beds and a zombie · fits in 16×7×10

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The columns are where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks: blue until one does, then yellow to red by how many have, and a flash at each spawn (Spawn spots hides them). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell pods under leaves; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water and the lava (16 bucketfuls) where the view shows them. On the first night, hide the zombie from the villagers by hand (a block in front of it) and take the block away in the morning: the villagers need one night's sleep before they summon the first golem.

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

The day-1 iron farm: for 11 iron (the shears for its leaves counted) and no quartz, three villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems that burn in lava pits, about 295 iron an hour (62% of the game's ceiling). Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell pods under leaves; a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes. Golems spawn in a row of 8 1x1 pits west of the zombie, a lava source beside their head on both sides, and burn where they stand. One tripwire runs through every pit at knee height: a golem in any pit opens the trapdoor in front of the zombie, which hides it, and the villagers calm down (at night they fall asleep) while the golem burns. A water channel under the string carries the iron to one hopper. Hide the zombie by hand on the first night: the villagers need a recent sleep to summon the first golem.
