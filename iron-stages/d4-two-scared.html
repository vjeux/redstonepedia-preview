Details: how it works in depth, our test, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm: the 3D view runs our test's own simulation of the game's mob rules, the very run our test made first, tick for tick. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Its numbers come from its kind's judge, run for this preview on our simulation of the game (every build, robust); it has not been tested in the real game. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
