Iron farm

882 iron an hour · 1,160 blocks · 0.76 an hour per block · 31 iron to build · 2 modules in a row send their golems to one kill chamber

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Its timing changes with where it is built in the world: try it where you build it.

92% of the game's maximum · a golem every 16 s · needs 8 villagers, 8 beds and 2 zombies · fits in 37×37×16

882 iron an hourIron farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT collects the first copy's iron; every copy has its own chest under its last hopper. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: 2 farms side by side, 24 blocks apart, each with its own villagers, zombie and comparator-ring timer; their golems fall to one water channel deep below every farm's sight, which carries them to one lava chamber and one chest.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water and the lava (4 bucketfuls) where the view shows them.

Blocks used: chest cobblestone cobblestone slab comparator hopper lava oak boat oak fence gate oak trapdoor observer red bed redstone wire repeater villager water

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT collects the first copy's iron; every copy has its own chest under its last hopper. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
