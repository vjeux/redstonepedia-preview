Iron farm

fits in 37×37×16 · 1,160 blocks

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Iron farmIron farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: 2 farms side by side, 24 blocks apart, each with its own villagers, zombie and comparator-ring timer; their golems fall to one water channel deep below every farm's sight, which carries them to one lava chamber and one chest.

In depth

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. It has no numbers yet. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
