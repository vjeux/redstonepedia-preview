Iron farm

428 iron an hour · 392 blocks · 1.1 an hour per block · 16 iron to build · golems fall out of sight to a lava blade

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

In rain it makes 448 iron an hour and in thunder 418, against 448 on a clear day. Lightning breaks it about once every 1,284 game days (428 hours of play), most likely by turning a villager into a witch.

90% of the game's maximum · a golem every 34 s · needs 4 villagers, 4 beds and a zombie · starts again by itself after a missed night · fits in 10×29×16

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The magenta floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Golems fall out of sight to a lava blade.

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water and the lava (4 bucketfuls) where the view shows them. Build it facing the way the download does, without turning it: turned another way it stops or changes its timing.

Blocks used: chest cobblestone cobblestone slab comparator hopper lava oak boat oak fence gate oak trapdoor observer red bed redstone wire repeater villager water

Details: how it works, measurements and properties

In depth

villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
