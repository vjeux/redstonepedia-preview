Day-1 iron farm (iron armour on your first day)

295 iron an hour · 204 blocks · 1.4 an hour per block · 18 iron to build · golems burn in lava pits

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

To build it: 18 iron in hand, 16 buckets of lava and 3 villagers from the village. Hide the zombie by hand the first night. Needs the zombie hidden by hand after days away.

51 minutes from the first block to a full set of iron armour, then 295 iron an hour

Build it

Download .litematic Download the design as text

The .litematic opens in Litematica (Minecraft 26.3): every block of the design in one region. The text file is the same build written as plain text, block by block.

Pour in the water and the lava (16 bucketfuls) where the view shows them. On the first night, hide the zombie from the villagers by hand (a block in front of it) and take the block away in the morning: the villagers need one night's sleep before they summon the first golem.

Notes from its designer

The day-1 iron farm, with no quartz and no shears: three villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems that burn in lava pits. Three villagers sleep on their beds in closed 1-cell pods under cobblestone roofs (nothing a lightning bolt can set on fire); a zombie in a boat 7 blocks north is in view of their standing and sleeping eyes. Golems spawn in a row of 8 1x1 pits west of the zombie, a lava source beside their head on both sides, and burn where they stand. One tripwire runs through every pit at knee height: a golem in any pit opens the trapdoor in front of the zombie, which hides it, and the villagers calm down (at night they fall asleep) while the golem burns. A water channel under the string carries the iron to one hopper. Hide the zombie by hand on the first night: the villagers need a recent sleep to summon the first golem.
