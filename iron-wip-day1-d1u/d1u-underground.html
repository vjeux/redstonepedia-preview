Iron farm

252 iron an hour · 299 blocks · 0.84 an hour per block · 8 iron to build · golems burn in a corner of their pit

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

In rain it makes 252 iron an hour and in thunder 249, against 256 on a clear day. Lightning breaks it about once every 7,233 game days (2,411 hours of play), most likely by a fire.

53% of the game's maximum · a golem every 57 s · needs 3 villagers, 3 beds and a zombie · starts again by itself after a missed night · fits in 6×19×16

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The columns are where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks: blue until one does, then yellow to red by how many have, and a flash at each spawn (Spawn spots hides them). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: Day-1 iron farm dug into the ground beside a village: two villagers on their beds watch a zombie parked in a boat and keep summoning iron golems.

How to try it

Press play to run the farm. The villagers sleep in their beds at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn, fall and die in the lava, and the hoppers carry their iron and poppies to the chest. The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. The top right shows the game's time, the golems so far and the iron in the chest; the speed menu plays it faster or slower. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Notes from its designer

Day-1 iron farm dug into the ground beside a village: two villagers on their beds watch a zombie parked in a boat and keep summoning iron golems. At nightfall a third villager lies down in its bed, its pressure plate lets go and a trapdoor hides the zombie: the two fall asleep, then see it again from their beds and wake scared, so golems come day and night. Golems appear on a water pad 7 blocks down, fall through its middle into a pit and burn in its corner under one lava source; the iron drops into a hopper and a chest at the bottom, reached by a staircase.
