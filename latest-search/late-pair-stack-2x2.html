Iron farm

fits in 11×126×19 · 1,668 blocks

Villagers scared by a zombie summon iron golems; the farm kills them and collects their iron in a chest.

Iron farmIron farm3D view loading (or not available in this browser)

Try it

Press play to watch it work: the villagers sleep at night and panic at the zombie by day, golems spawn and die, and the hoppers carry their iron to the chest; the top right counts the game's time, the golems and the iron. The blue floor is where a golem can spawn, seen through the blocks (Spawn spots hides it). The chest tagged OUT, under the last hopper, collects the iron. In the game, build it and wait near it.

How this design does it: 4 module(s); each: 3 scared and 0 blind villagers in 1-cell pods, a zombie in a boat, a bed observer and catalog timing part 0; golems spawn on a 6x6 pad 7 below the beds and fall through its dry hole into a fire-safe lava blade 22 below the beds.
